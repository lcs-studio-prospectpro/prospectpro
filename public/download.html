Getting started

  1. Don't have a ProspectPro account yet? Create one free — 14-day trial, or sign in with your existing account inside the desktop app.
  2. Install and open ProspectPro Desktop.
  3. Sign in, then go to Search and set your one region by county or zip code — that becomes your permanent desktop territory.
  4. Start searching, confirming, and logging calls — all synced to your ProspectPro account.
