Privacy Policy & Data Usage

Last updated: September 2026

ProspectPro ("we", "us", "the App") is a territory and prospect management tool for sales teams. This page explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, work email, password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text), and company name.
  • Prospect/contact data you enter or import: business names, contact names, titles, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and social links you add manually or via CSV import.
  • Activity data: call logs, notes, task assignments, and confirmation status your team records inside the App.
  • Search/territory data: the category, state, city, zip code, and radius you set on the Search page, used to organize your territory and, where possible, to estimate distance to your contacts.
  • Billing data: if you upgrade to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe — we do not store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To operate the core features of the App: organizing contacts by category/territory, tracking call activity, and managing your team's task queue.
  • To calculate approximate distances between your search territory and contacts that have location data, using a free public zip-code lookup service (no data broker or ad network is involved).
  • To send account-related emails (e.g., trial status) and to respond if you contact support.
  • We do not sell your data or your contacts' data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data storage & security

Your data is stored on our hosting provider's infrastructure and is only accessible to your company's own account (tenant) — other ProspectPro customers cannot see your contacts or activity. Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form. As with any early-stage product, no system is 100% guaranteed against all risk, and you should avoid storing highly sensitive personal data (e.g. government ID numbers, health information) in contact notes.

Your contacts' data

If you upload or enter information about business contacts (leads, prospects), you are responsible for having a lawful basis to contact them (e.g. legitimate business interest, opt-in, or existing relationship) under applicable laws such as CAN-SPAM, TCPA, and any state or regional privacy laws that apply to your business.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete contact records at any time inside the App.
  • You can request deletion of your company's account and all associated data by emailing us (see below).

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email prospectprosupport@gmail.com.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and is not a substitute for legal advice. If your business handles regulated data (e.g. health, financial, or data from EU/UK residents), please consult a qualified attorney to ensure full compliance.
